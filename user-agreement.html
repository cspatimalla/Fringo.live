User Agreement

Fringo.Live

This agreement explains the standards expected of everyone using Fringo.Live. Read it together with the Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

Our shared commitment

Fringo.Live supports respectful friendships and peaceful conversations. Every user helps keep the community welcoming.

Friendship requests

Respect the other person's decision. Do not pressure, repeatedly contact, threaten, or harass someone who declines or does not respond.

Words, images, and behaviour

  • Do not use vulgar, abusive, threatening, hateful, or harassing language.
  • Do not upload or share vulgar, sexually explicit, exploitative, or otherwise inappropriate images.
  • Do not bully, intimidate, impersonate, scam, or manipulate others.
  • Respect privacy and do not share another person's personal information without permission.

Audio and video calls

Use calls respectfully. Do not record, capture, or distribute another person's call content without appropriate consent and legal authority.

Reporting concerns

Use available reporting and blocking features if you encounter inappropriate conduct. Do not retaliate or publish private information.

Enforcement

Violations may result in content removal, restrictions, suspension, or account termination under platform policies and applicable law.

Contact and support

For questions about these community standards or to report a concern, contact supportfringo@gmail.com. You may also use reporting and blocking tools available in the app.
