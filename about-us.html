About Us

Fringo.Live

Our purpose

Fringo.Live is a friendship-focused platform created to help people connect in a positive and respectful environment. We believe a good conversation can make a difference: people can share thoughts, listen, and build genuine friendships.

How connections work

People can discover one another and send friendship requests. A friendship is established only when the recipient approves the request.

Communication features

  • One-to-one audio calling with an approved friend.
  • One-to-one video calling with an approved friend.

Our community standard

Vulgar or abusive words and vulgar or inappropriate images are not allowed. Users are expected to communicate respectfully and help maintain a peaceful community. Use the in-app reporting and blocking facilities where available.

Our aspiration

We want Fringo.Live to be a welcoming place where people can talk, listen, and form positive connections. It is not a substitute for professional counselling, healthcare, or emergency support.
